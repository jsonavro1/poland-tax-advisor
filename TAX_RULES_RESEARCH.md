## Profile and scope assumptions

Unless a rule below says otherwise, “standard employee” means an adult employee with one ordinary employment relationship, remuneration subject to the general Polish tax scale, and no special relief, joint assessment, additional income, or exceptional contribution status. A person’s actual eligibility and payroll outcome can differ.

- Monthly salary and annual tax are distinct: payroll advances are not necessarily the final annual liability.
- Standard employee tax-deductible costs (KUP) assume the ordinary workplace arrangement specified in the rule; commuter and multiple-employer variants are noted separately.
- Standard PPK means the statutory basic contributions only; PPK participation by the employee is voluntary. State subsidies are conditional.
- The ordinary IKZE limit is for a saver who is not entitled to the higher limit for a person conducting qualifying non-agricultural business activity.
- No gross-to-net or tax calculation is performed in this document.

### Implementation traceability

The verified PPK records used by the current application are stored separately from calculation code in `src/rules/index.ts`:

- `ppk.employee.basic-contribution` — Act on Employee Capital Plans, Art. 27(1), 2% of the gross PPK remuneration base.
- `ppk.employer.basic-contribution` — Act on Employee Capital Plans, Art. 26(1), 1.5% of the gross PPK remuneration base.
- `ppk.state.welcome-payment` — Act on Employee Capital Plans, Art. 31(1)–(3), PLN 250 subject to statutory and participant-history conditions.
- `ppk.state.annual-payment` — Act on Employee Capital Plans, Art. 32(1)–(6), PLN 240 subject to statutory thresholds and exclusions.

Each record stores its source URL, cited legal basis, applicable 2026 period, access date `2026-10-27`, value/unit, and `VERIFIED_CURRENT` status. The statutory payment amounts are not treated as automatic entitlements; the UI requires the user to confirm eligibility conditions and labels that confirmation as user-provided. This does not close G-1 through G-5 or G-7.

### PIT blockers not closed by this pass

The Ministry of Finance rates page gives the candidate 12%/32% general scale and PLN 3,600 reduction; the current PIT consolidation Dz.U. 2026 poz. 592, Art. 27(1), contains that wording.
