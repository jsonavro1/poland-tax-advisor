# Polish UoP Tax & Pension Rules — Research

**Research scope:** Standard employee working in Poland under an employment contract (*umowa o pracę*, UoP)  
**Tax year reviewed:** 2026  
**Sources accessed:** 2026-10-27  
**Purpose:** Research notes for product planning, not implementation instructions or individual tax advice.

## Important verification note

Second-pass review of the requested UoP blockers was performed on **2026-10-27** by opening and inspecting the official source pages and the full official Journal of Laws PDFs listed below. The sources establish that the candidate values occur in the cited statutory texts or portal explanations. However, the inspected PIT, social-insurance, health-insurance, and PPK legal consolidations predate tax year 2026, and the general official pages inspected do not expressly state that all of those values and conditions apply in 2026. Under the requested standard, **none of the requested blocker values is verified for tax year 2026**. Keep each blocked even where the published text supports the candidate value for its own consolidation date.

The 2025 PIT consolidated text, Dz.U. 2025 poz. 163, contains the candidate scale and employee-cost text, but does not itself establish that the terms were unchanged throughout 2026. The 2025 Social Insurance System Act consolidation and the 2024 healthcare and PPK consolidations likewise do not by themselves establish 2026 applicability. Do not activate any such value in a calculation engine or substitute a remembered value.

## Profile and scope assumptions

Unless a rule below says otherwise, “standard employee” means an adult employee with one ordinary employment relationship, remuneration subject to the general Polish tax scale, and no special relief, joint assessment, additional income, or exceptional contribution status. A person’s actual eligibility and payroll outcome can differ.

- Monthly salary and annual tax are distinct: payroll advances are not necessarily the final annual liability.
- Standard employee tax-deductible costs (KUP) assume the ordinary workplace arrangement specified in the rule; commuter and multiple-employer variants are noted separately.
- Standard PPK means the statutory basic contributions only; PPK participation by the employee is voluntary. State subsidies are conditional.
- The ordinary IKZE limit is for a saver who is not entitled to the higher limit for a person conducting qualifying non-agricultural business activity.
- No gross-to-net or tax calculation is performed in this document.

## Supporting official-source notes

- **ZUS 2026 limit:** ZUS’s 2026 information says the annual pension/disability base ceiling is PLN 282,600 and cites the 2025-11-19 Ministerial notice, Monitor Polski poz. 1206. The page was accessed 2026-10-27.
- **IKZE 2026 cap:** The MRPiPS page states PLN 11,304 for ordinary savers and PLN 16,956 for people conducting qualifying non-agricultural business activity. It cites the notice of 2025-11-10, Monitor Polski poz. 1156. Accessed 2026-10-27.
- **PPK values and conditions:** The official MojePPK FAQ was opened and read directly. It states the 2% employee and at least 1.5% employer basic payments, PLN 250 welcome payment, and PLN 240 annual payment. The PPK Act consolidation at Dz.U. 2024 poz. 427 was also opened as an official Journal of Laws PDF and Articles 26, 27, 31, and 32 inspected. These are source-text findings, not verification of 2026 applicability.
- **IKZE taxation:** The official MRPiPS IKZE overview describes deduction of contributions from the PIT base and the 10% lump-sum tax on payout after the saving period; it also distinguishes early refund taxation under the PIT scale. Accessed 2026-10-27.
- **PIT consolidated text limitation:** ELI identifies Dz.U. 2025 poz. 163 as the consolidated text of the PIT Act and reports that the announcement was no longer in force from 2026-04-29. The Act is amended over time; the historical consolidation is not by itself proof that every cited PIT value remained unchanged through 2026-10-27.
- **Employee social-insurance share allocation:** The employee percentages above are the insured-person shares prescribed for the employment relationship by the Social Insurance System Act; the ZUS 2026 information page is primarily organized around payer/self-employed contribution calculations and is not, on its own, a complete employee payroll specification.
